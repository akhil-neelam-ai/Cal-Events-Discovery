About CalEvents

CalEvents gathers UC Berkeley's public event calendars into one feed you can search. It updates every morning.

Where events come from

Each morning CalEvents reads these public calendars, removes duplicates and sorts every event into a category.

  • UC Berkeley Events, the official campus calendar, with its department and student-life calendars
  • Cal Performances
  • Cal Bears Athletics
  • BAMPFA, the campus art museum and film archive
  • Berkeley Haas
  • Berkeley Law
  • Simons Institute
  • Berkeley Gateway to Innovation
  • Berkeley AI Risk speaker series
  • Berkeley Risk and Security Lab
  • Center for Long-Term Cybersecurity
  • Luma calendars run by Berkeley groups

Every event links to its original listing. Times and places can change, so check that listing before you go.

Who runs it

Akhil Neelam, an MBA student at Berkeley Haas, built CalEvents and keeps it running. It is an independent student project, not an official UC Berkeley service.

The code is public on GitHub.
