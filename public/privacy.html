Privacy

CalEvents has no accounts, no ads and no sign-up. It measures how the site is used with three analytics tools, and one of them sets cookies.

Last updated November 19, 2026

What gets recorded

When you use the site, it records:

  • the page you opened and the site that sent you here
  • what you type in the search box
  • the filters you pick and the events you open
  • the buttons and links you click, including links to an event's own page
  • your browser, device type and screen size
  • your rough location, such as your country or city

None of this is tied to your name or email. The site never asks for either.

The three tools

Google Analytics

Counts visits and records the activity above, including search terms.

Cookies: yes. Cookies named _ga tell one visitor from another and last up to two years.

PostHog

Records the same activity. It does not record your screen.

Cookies: none. It tells visitors apart with a scrambled ID that resets every day.

Vercel Web Analytics

Counts page views, referring sites, countries and device types.

Cookies: none.

What stays on your device

Your recent searches are saved in your browser so the search box can offer them again. That list never leaves your device. Your browser also remembers when you dismiss a tip.

Others that see your visit

  • Vercel hosts the site and, like any host, logs requests along with their network addresses.
  • Event pages, calendar links and map links open other sites, which have their own policies.

Your choices

  • Blocking cookies or using a tracker blocker stops Google Analytics. Google also offers an opt-out add-on.
  • PostHog and Vercel Web Analytics load from this site's own address, so most blockers do not stop them. Neither uses cookies or builds a profile of you.
  • Clearing this site's data in your browser removes your recent searches.
